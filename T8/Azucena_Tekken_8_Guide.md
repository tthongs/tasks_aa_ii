# Tekken 8: Azucena Comprehensive Character & Move Guide

## 1. Character Overview & Identity

Azucena Milagros Ortiz Castillo, known as the "Coffee Queen" from Peru, is a high-energy Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) fighter whose combat rhythm combines Peruvian dance steps, snappy boxing slips, and relentless stance rushdown. In Tekken 8, Azucena stands out as a fluid, deceptive offensive force centered on psychological mind games, evasive stances, and suffocating frame advantage.

1. Libertador (LIB) Stance: A fluid sway stance that automatically evades incoming high and low attacks, completely invalidating conventional jab checks and low pokes.
2. Backturn (BT) Stance: Seamless spin transitions from neutral pokes, offering evasive rolls, jumping stomps, and crushing counter-hit launchers.
3. Relentless Close-Range Poking: Natural-combo strings, safe mid checks, and unreactable low chips that continuously force opponents to guess.
4. Devastating Whiff & Block Punishment: Immediate i10 wall-splatting punishers (1,2), i14 Heat Engager launchers (3,3), and long-range lunging launchers (df+2, f,f+3).

## 2. Tekken 8 System Mechanics & What Defines Azucena

1. Libertador (LIB) Auto-Evasion:
   - While idle or swaying in Libertador (f+3+4), Azucena automatically ducks under high attacks and hops over low attacks without requiring defensive inputs.
   - Opponents attempting to poke their way out with jabs or low checks get caught in her automatic counter-strikes.
   - Counterplay & Vulnerabilities: LIB stance does not auto-evade mid attacks or throws unless specifically modified during Heat.
2. Backturn (BT) Mobility & Cancels:
   - Many of Azucena's fundamental pokes transition into Backturn by holding back (hold b) or natively on hit (e.g., 4,1, db+3, df+1,4 into b).
   - From BT, Azucena can perform evasive jumps (BT f+3+4 low crush), backwards retreat rolls (BT b+3+4 high crush), or armored strikes (BT 1+2).
3. While Running 3,2 (f,f,f+3,2) Pressure:
   - Azucena's signature lunging attack. On block, it pushes the opponent back with massive frame advantage (+5), forcing an immediate stance guessing game.
4. Heat-Enhanced Libertador & Health Recovery:
   - In Heat State, entering Libertador stance accelerates her rhythm, grants enhanced auto-parries, and recovers grey health upon successful evasions and hits.

## 4. Key Pokes & Neutral Game

### Mid Pokes & Spacing
- df+1 / df+1,4 (i13 Mid Check):
  - The cornerstone of Azucena's neutral. At -1 on block and +5 on hit, it keeps opponent offense completely in check.
  - String extensions include df+1,4 (natural mid-high), df+1,4 into b (enters Backturn), and df+1,4,1 (delayed mid bait).
- b+1 (i14 Safe Mid Strike):
  - Fast mid check that is safe on block (-4). On Counter Hit, it causes a crumple stun for a full combo.
- f+3 / f+3,4 (i16 Mid Knee & High Kick):
  - Long-range mid knee. Vital combo filler and spacing check that stops advancing opponents.
- uf+4 (Orbital Heel - Safe Low Crush):
  - Classic low-crush orbital kick. Safe on block (-4) and yields a guaranteed f,f+3 or db+1+2 on clean hit.
- uf+1 (Punch Sabaki Parry):
  - Built-in parry active on startup against high and mid punches. Parrying an opponent's punch guarantees f,f+3 for 42+ free damage.

### Low Pokes & Mixups
- db+3 / db+3,4 (i18 Low Stature Kick):
  - Azucena's most vital low poke. On normal hit, leaves her at +4 in Backturn (BT), establishing an immediate 50/50 mixup between BT 1+2 (mid/armor) and BT 3 (low sweep).
  - Pressing 4 (db+3,4) executes a high kick that launches for a Tornado combo if the opponent tries to interrupt.
- d+4 / d+4,1 (i12 Fast Low Interrupt):
  - Swift unreactable low kick. d+4,1 is a natural combo low-high; holding f transitions seamlessly into Libertador.
- LIB d+4,4 (Libertador Low Sweep):
  - Heavy low from stance that chips opponent health and launches for a full juggle on Counter Hit.

### Approach & Space Control
- f,f,f+3,2 (While Running 3,2):
  - The premier approach tool in Tekken 8. Pushes opponent deep into block stun at +5 frame advantage, setting up guaranteed stance pressure.
- f,f+3 (Lunging Mid Kick):
  - Safe-on-block mid keep-out tool that launches for a full combo on normal hit.

      v                   v
[ Libertador (LIB) ] <===> [ Backturn (BT) ]

## 6. Heat System Mechanics

- Heat Engagers:
  - 3,3 (i14 Standing Punisher)
  - f+1+2 (Lunging Mid Shoulder)
  - db+1+2 (Heavy Ground Slam)
  - BT 1+2 (Backturned Armored Strike)
  - LIB 1+2 (Libertador Mid Launcher)
  - Heat Burst (2+3)
- Heat State Properties:
  - Libertador stance auto-dodges become faster and restore recoverable grey health.
  - Heat Dash (f,f after any Heat Engager) grants +5 frame advantage on block or converts into a devastating full juggle combo on hit.
- Heat Smash (2+3 during Heat):
  - Cinematic high-damage strike where Azucena dances through the opponent's defenses, dealing 50+ unscaled damage and resetting neutral.

## 9. Beginner-to-Intermediate Pickup Blueprint

If you are picking up Azucena today, follow this 4-step game plan:

### Step 1: Establish Neutral Dominance (Range 2 to 3)
- Open rounds with f,f,f+3,2 (Running 3,2). On block, you are at +5.
- If the opponent tries to stay back, check them with f,f+3 (lunging mid launcher) or close in with df+1.
- Whiff punish opponents with df+2 (i15 uppercut) or 3,3 (Heat Engager).

### Step 2: The Stance Flowchart & Mind Games (Range 0 to 1)
- Poke with df+1 and db+3:
  - db+3 hits low and puts you in Backturn (BT) at +4. Immediately press BT 1+2 (armored mid) or BT 3 (low sweep).
- Enter Libertador (LIB) via 1,1 into f or d+2,3:
  - If they mash jabs or low kicks: Do nothing! Your auto-dodge will evade them automatically.
  - If they stand and guard: Hit them with LIB 3,1 (low-high) or LIB 2+3 (command throw).
  - If they duck: Blast them with LIB 1+2 (mid launcher) into Combo 07!

### Step 3: Defense & Evasion
- Punish unsafe blocked attacks instantly:
  - Any -10 move: Punish with 1,2 (wall splats and leaves you +8!).
  - Any -14 move: Punish with 3,3 into Heat Dash!
  - Blocked Hell Sweeps / Snake Edges: Punish with ws2 into Combo 03!
- When trapped under opponent punch pressure, use uf+1 (Sabaki punch parry) for a free f,f+3 knockdown!

### Step 4: Convert Every Launch to the Wall
- Use the Accessible BnB (Combo 02) to build muscle memory:
  df+2 -> 4,1 -> BT 3 -> LIB 4,1,2 (Tornado) -> dash -> f+2,1,4
- Upgrade to the Optimal BnB (Combo 01):
  df+2 -> df+3 -> d+2,3 -> LIB 3+4 -> micro dash -> db+3,4 (Tornado) -> dash -> f+2,1,4
- At the wall, finish with df+1,4 -> 1,2,1!
